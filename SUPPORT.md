## 🆘 Common Issues & Solutions

### Account & Login Issues
**Problem:** Can't log in to my account
**Solution:** 
1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify your email address is correct
3. Try resetting your password
4. Clear app cache and restart
5. Contact support if issues persist

**Problem:** Account not recognizing premium status
**Solution:**
1. Ensure you're logged in with the correct account
2. Check your subscription status in App Store/Google Play
3. Try logging out and back in
4. Restore purchases if available
5. Contact support with your purchase receipt

### App Performance Issues
**Problem:** App crashes or freezes
**Solution:**
1. Close and restart the app
2. Restart your device
3. Update to the latest app version
4. Clear app cache
5. Check available storage space

**Problem:** Slow loading or connection issues
**Solution:**
1. Check your internet connection
2. Try switching between WiFi and mobile data
3. Restart your router/modem
4. Close other apps using internet
5. Check if your device has sufficient storage

### Subscription & Billing Issues
**Problem:** Subscription not working
**Solution:**
1. Verify subscription is active in App Store/Google Play
2. Check payment method is valid
3. Try restoring purchases
4. Contact support with purchase details

**Problem:** Charged but no premium access
**Solution:**
1. Wait 5-10 minutes for payment to process
2. Log out and back in
3. Restore purchases
4. Contact support with transaction ID

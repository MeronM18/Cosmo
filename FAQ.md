# Cosmo App - Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Quick Answers to Common Questions**

## 📱 General App Questions

### Q: What is Cosmo?
A: Cosmo is a personalized astrology app that provides birth chart analysis, daily horoscopes, moon phase tracking, and astrological insights based on your birth information.

### Q: How much does Cosmo cost?
A: Cosmo offers both free and premium features:
- **Free:** Basic horoscopes, simple birth chart, and limited features
- **Premium:** Advanced birth chart analysis, detailed readings, compatibility reports, and exclusive content
- **Pricing:** Monthly subscription ($9.99/month) or Annual subscription ($79.99/year)

### Q: Is my data safe and private?
A: Yes! We take privacy seriously:
- All data is encrypted and securely stored
- We never sell your personal information
- You can delete your account and data at any time
- See our Privacy Policy for complete details

### Q: Can I use Cosmo on multiple devices?
A: Yes! Your account syncs across all your devices. Simply log in with the same account on any supported device.

## 🔐 Account & Login

### Q: How do I create an account?
A: 
1. Download the Cosmo app
2. Tap "Sign Up" or "Create Account"
3. Choose your preferred sign-in method (Apple, Google, or Email)
4. Enter your birth information (date, time, location)
5. Complete your profile setup

### Q: I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
A: 
1. On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password"
2. Enter your email address
3. Check your email for reset instructions
4. Follow the link to create a new password

### Q: Can I change my birth information after creating my account?
A: Yes! You can update your birth information in the app settings. However, this will recalculate all your astrological data.

### Q: Why do you need my exact birth time and location?
A: Accurate birth time and location are essential for precise astrological calculations. Even a few minutes difference can significantly change your birth chart and readings.

## 💳 Subscription & Billing

### Q: How do I subscribe to premium features?
A: 
1. Open the app and tap "Upgrade to Premium"
2. Choose your subscription plan (Monthly or Annual)
3. Complete the purchase through your App Store or Google Play account
4. Your premium features will be activated immediately

### Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: 
- **iOS:** Go to Settings > [Your Name] > Subscriptions > Cosmo > Cancel Subscription
- **Android:** Open Google Play Store > Menu > Subscriptions > Cosmo > Cancel
- You'll retain access until the end of your current billing period

### Q: Will I be charged if I cancel my subscription?
A: No! You won't be charged again after cancellation. You'll keep premium access until your current period ends.

### Q: Can I get a refund?
A: Refunds are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. Contact them directly for refund requests. We may assist with technical issues that prevent app usage.

### Q: What happens if I don't renew my subscription?
A: Your account will revert to free features. Your data and account will remain intact, but you'll lose access to premium content.

## 🔧 Technical Issues

### Q: The app is crashing. What should I do?
A: Try these steps:
1. Close and restart the app
2. Restart your device
3. Update to the latest app version
4. Clear app cache
5. If problems persist, contact support

### Q: The app is loading slowly. How can I fix this?
A: 
1. Check your internet connection
2. Close other apps using internet
3. Try switching between WiFi and mobile data
4. Restart your router/modem
5. Ensure you have sufficient storage space

### Q: I'm not getting notifications. How do I enable them?
A: 
1. Go to your device Settings
2. Find Cosmo in your app list
3. Enable Notifications
4. Also check the app's notification settings

### Q: The app won't load my birth chart. What's wrong?
A: 
1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify your birth information is correct
3. Try logging out and back in
4. Clear app cache and restart
5. Contact support if issues persist
